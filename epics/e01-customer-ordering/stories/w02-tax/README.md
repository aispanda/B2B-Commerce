## Solution and decisions

Retain net pricing and use the native Salesforce Tax calculation already configured in the test context, subject to fresh access/mode checks. Reuse standard cart/checkout/order summaries where they actually work. For browse estimates, investigate a supported calculation path with explicit product, price, quantity, buyer and destination; absent or failed context must not appear as a true $0. One historical stateless REST probe returned `INVALID_API_INPUT`, and a non-commit Apex diagnostic produced a null-pointer exception with undetermined cause; neither produced an estimate. The documented REST address input exposes country/state, so do not assert exact local-tax precision. Do not use a mocked Stripe product-tax GET response as a live rate.

Carry forward the original context-change contract referenced by AC-T03: recalculate for **account, destination, variant, quantity, discounts, currency and shipping** changes. Include product classification, seller registration and buyer exemption context when establishing an appropriate tax basis. Use the buyer-selected delivery destination where available; otherwise require destination input or a separately approved, clearly labeled fictional sample. Do not silently substitute store country or billing address. Show estimated tax with destination and unit/quantity basis; when destination is unavailable, provide an actionable prompt to enter it. Explain a genuine zero or exemption when applicable. For PDP use the selected sellable variant and quantity; for PLP/search state a quantity-one sellable-item basis. Unresolved parent variants require selection or an explicitly supported range. Distinguish estimated and final values; confirmation and order detail use persisted submitted-order tax. Do not turn provider errors into a zero-tax result or share cached buyer/exemption data across accounts. Any proposed batching, limits, timeouts and caching remain unverified design hypotheses.

The native manual solution is documented for gross taxation and is not selected to replace the net-price requirement. Simulated tax and any flat table remain unselected alternatives requiring the original scope/label decisions; no free, accurate end-to-end service or cost entitlement was proven. Provider costs, category-specific taxability, exemptions, retry/latency budgets, full destination handling, published-to-draft parity and rollback remain pending.

Public references: [Salesforce B2B tax setup](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=commerce.comm_setuptaxes.htm&type=5) distinguishes supported approaches; [Commerce Tax APIs](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/commerce/salesforce-commerce/guide/b2b-d2c-comm-tax-apis.html) describes off-cart POST and the Stripe GET limitation; [Cart Calculate API](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/commerce/salesforce-commerce/guide/cart-calculate-api.html) describes calculation orchestration; [Stripe Tax testing](https://docs.stripe.com/tax/testing) describes test behavior. These sources do not prove this org's entitlement, current behavior or real-world rate accuracy.
