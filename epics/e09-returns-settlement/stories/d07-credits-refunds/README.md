## Solution and perspectives

Use Salesforce's standard **Create Invoice from Fulfillment Order** Flow action to prepare the invoice fixture, then use standard managed RMA and credit processing after readiness is established. The action takes a fulfillment order, rather than an individual return unit; validate all included lines and the full invoice total before running. Salesforce documents `Fulfillment Order Id` as the input and an Apex-defined invoice creation output whose `invoiceId` identifies the created invoice. The target fulfillment order must not already have an invoice. These are documented action semantics; the org's behavior remains unverified. A manual, explicitly invoked demo flow is the proposed design; its final trigger, access, input validation, duplicate prevention, error handling and safe execution must be verified before use. Do not add an Ensure Funds or payment-capture action. After creation, verify the invoice and each relevant invoice line, fulfillment-order link, status, currency and unpaid balance against the baseline. Map the invoice line to its source order item and the unit intended for return; preserve evidence linking the later RMA, returned item, credit memo and invoice. Invoice creation does not prove PO terms or establish the behavior of RMA close. Financial preflight precedes RMA close, because closing can itself trigger credit/refund processing. Reconcile actual financial records and process exceptions before retrying an uncertain operation. The separate immediate-return option is not the receipt-first D05 scenario.

Design sources: [Create Invoice from Fulfillment Order](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=platform.flow_ref_elements_om_actions_create_invoice_from_fo.htm&type=5) describes the standard action and fulfillment-order input; [InvoiceLine reference](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/object-reference/references/standard-objects/object-invoiceline.html) describes invoice lines derived from order lines; [Order Summary relationships](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/commerce/salesforce-order-management/guide/order-management-order-summary-erd.html) depicts related Order Management objects; [Billing Invoice data model](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/data-models/guide/billing-invoice.html) depicts the broader Revenue Management billing model. These sources do not prove this org's configuration or test results.

Historical official references: [managed RMA close](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=commerce.om_close_a_managed_rma_return_order.htm&language=en_US&type=5), [Order Management flows](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=commerce.om_flows.htm&language=en_US&type=5), [payments and invoices](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.om_payments.htm&language=en_US&type=5), and [OrderSummary operations](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/apex-ref/references/apex-ref/apex-connectapi-ordersummary-static-methods.html).
