# Journeys, personas and candidate requirements

All entries in this file are **proposed discovery prompts**, generalized from the [Agentforce workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1_qaxZ0e2-dJEHFQH5eOyza9vYak1UBux/edit) Requirements and Coverage Matrix tabs and the [journey inventory](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nXyqEq3NlDQK2LpGr0ly8qZCiu3MxmEpOkMoc2DyYOo/edit) Scenarios tab. They are not universal customer needs, approved policies or verified product features. References to row ranges let an authorized reader trace the source. Role names can overlap; a person may have more than one role.

## Personas

| ID | Persona | Job and goal | Common need or pain point to validate |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | Buyer / procurement user | Find authorized products and buy or reorder for an organization. | Correct account, contract price, package, PO and delivery; avoid duplicate orders. |
| P02 | Buyer administrator / approver | Manage purchasing access and approvals. | Know who can act for which account and how requests progress. |
| P03 | Clinician / product evaluator | Evaluate product details and compatibility for an intended use. | Reliable, current specifications and safety information; clinical decisions remain with qualified professionals. |
| P04 | Receiving / inventory / consignment operator | Receive, count, use and replenish supplies at a location. | Accurate stock, lot or serial details where needed, and correction of discrepancies. |
| P05 | Customer service agent | Identify an authorized customer, resolve order issues and hand off exceptions. | Current order and shipment state, policy, safe actions and clear ownership. |
| P06 | Fulfillment / returns operator | Allocate, ship, receive and inspect eligible items. | Split shipments, damaged goods, return authorization and discrepancies. |
| P07 | Finance / accounts payable | Reconcile invoices, credits, payments and refunds. | Authoritative records, references, payment state and settlement discrepancies. |
| P08 | Equipment owner / service coordinator | Register equipment, request service and follow warranty, repair or replacement. | Asset and serial identification, coverage, quotes and service status. |
| P09 | Visitor, patient or caregiver | Find appropriate information or a route to help, where supported. | Accessible navigation, privacy, appropriate product and clinical guidance. Do not assume these roles can buy in a B2B store. |
| P10 | Merchandiser / commerce administrator | Maintain catalog, eligibility, pricing and approved offers. | Accurate content, account segmentation and changes that propagate safely. |
| P11 | Integration / operations owner | Connect systems and recover exceptions. | Correlation, traceability, safe retries and clear system ownership. |

## Suggested acceptance questions

For each selected requirement identify actor, trigger, permitted account, source of truth, preconditions, happy path, exception, outcome and owner. Test denied cross-account access, no results, stale data, duplicate requests, asynchronous failure and human handoff where relevant. Do not copy customer-specific values into this baseline.
