# B2B medical supplier customer and partner model

Use Salesforce `Account` records for the hospital system, its clinics, the invoice recipient, and the payer. Use `Account.ParentId` for organizational hierarchy. For each sold-to and SAP sales area, keep a reusable list of eligible partners and defaults. When an order is submitted, retain the selected partner identities and delivery and billing addresses with that transaction.

This public draft uses fictional records and documented Salesforce and SAP schema. Target-org findings are recorded with the source design.

The industry models below add their own sales-organization capabilities. [Sales Cloud data model](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/data-models/guide/sales-cloud-overview.html)

## Fictional example and creation order

Salesforce’s **Create a Sales Org** instructions apply to the Consumer Goods Cloud Enhanced Data Model. The documented setup requires Enterprise or Unlimited Edition with Consumer Goods Cloud enabled, a qualifying CGCloud admin permission, and configured sales-org and business-area picklist values. Its model uses `cgcloud__Sales_Organization__c` for business segmentation and `cgcloud__Sales_Organization_User__c` to map users to those segments. For the example below, preserve the full SAP sales-area key in the proposed integration model. [Create a Sales Org](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=ind.cg_task_admin_create_sales_org.htm&language=en_US&type=5), [Sales Organization Key Objects](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=ind.cg_reference_admin_sales_org_unit_key_objects.htm&language=en_US&type=5)

**1. Create the business accounts and contacts.** The keys below are fictional SAP customer numbers. Use `Account.Name` for the names and a proposed external-ID mapping for SAP keys. Create the hospital before the two clinics so their `Account.ParentId` can reference it. Set each contact’s `FirstName`, `LastName`, `Title`, and `AccountId`.

| SAP customer | `Account.Name` | `Account.ParentId` | Contact on that account |
|---|---|---|---|
| `0000100100` | Cedar Vale Health System | — | Maya Chen, Procurement Lead |
| `0000100110` | Cedar Vale North Clinic | Cedar Vale Health System | Eli Brooks, Receiving Lead |
| `0000100120` | Cedar Vale East Clinic | Cedar Vale Health System | Nora Diaz, Receiving Lead |
| `0000100190` | Cedar Vale Shared Billing | — | Priya Shah, Invoice Coordinator |
| `0000100195` | Harbor Treasury Services | — | Theo Reed, Payments Analyst |

In this example Shared Billing and Harbor Treasury are standalone service entities that can serve multiple customers. The proposed partner assignment can reference an operational contact, so East’s ship-to assignment points to Nora and the bill-to assignment points to Priya. If Maya also needs a CRM relationship to East Clinic, a future `AccountContactRelation` would use Maya’s `ContactId` and East’s `AccountId`, with `IsDirect=false` set by Salesforce. That step depends on enabling Contacts to Multiple Accounts. [Account Contact Relationship fields](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/field-reference/references/field-reference/salesforce-field-reference-accountcontactrelation.html)

**2. Load the sales areas and partner assignments.** For the example, one sales organization, `1000` (US Medical), has two sales areas. A proposed customer sales-area record links Cedar Vale to the combination of sales organization, distribution channel, and division. Each proposed partner assignment links that record to the partner account and stores the function, SAP partner counter, and default flag. Preserve codes and counters as text so leading zeros survive.

| Sales area | Sold-to `AG` | Ship-to `WE` | Bill-to `RE` | Payer `RG` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `1000 / 10 / 01` · Direct | Cedar Vale Health System | North `001` default, East `002` alternate | Cedar Vale Shared Billing | Harbor Treasury Services |
| `1000 / 20 / 01` · Contract | Cedar Vale Health System | East `001` default | Cedar Vale Shared Billing | Harbor Treasury Services |

SAP’s Business Partner API can supply customer and business-partner IDs, addresses, contacts, and sales-area data. `A_CustSalesPartnerFunc` supplies `Customer`, `SalesOrganization`, `DistributionChannel`, `Division`, `PartnerFunction`, and `PartnerCounter` as the assignment key, plus `BPCustomerNumber` and `DefaultPartner`. Salesforce needs integration or custom logic to apply the relevant partner rules if selection happens in Salesforce. SAP can also determine or validate partners when it accepts the order. [SAP Business Partner API](https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_CLOUD/3c916ef10fc240c9afc594b346ffaf77/85043858ea0f9244e10000000a4450e5.html), [SAP customer sales partner functions](https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_CLOUD/3c916ef10fc240c9afc594b346ffaf77/7a61045826552246e10000000a441470.html), [SAP partner determination](https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_ON-PREMISE/7b24a64d9d0941bda1afa753263d9e39/806fbd534f22b44ce10000000a174cb4.html)

**3. Add the Commerce records.** Enable Cedar Vale as a buyer, then link its `Account.Id` to a buyer group assigned to the demo store. Under Cedar Vale’s `Account.Id`, create the following `ContactPointAddress` records using `Name`, `Street`, `City`, `StateCode`, `PostalCode`, and `CountryCode`. For the example, the checkout logic maps each eligible partner to its corresponding saved address.

| `Name` | `Street`, `City`, `StateCode`, `PostalCode`, `CountryCode` | `AddressType` | `IsDefault` |
|---|---|---|---|
| North Receiving | 110 Training Way, Atlanta, GA, 30309, US | `Shipping` | `true` |
| East Receiving | 120 Training Way, Atlanta, GA, 30307, US | `Shipping` | `false` |
| Shared Billing | 190 Training Way, Atlanta, GA, 30303, US | `Billing` | `true` |

`IsDefault` is a saved-address setting. The sales-area default comes from the partner assignment, so Contract orders default to East even though North is the saved default. Leave `BuyerAccount.SendToId` blank in this example because the default varies by sales area. [ContactPointAddress fields](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/field-reference/references/field-reference/salesforce-field-reference-contactpointaddress.html)

**4. Capture one order.** Maya places a Direct order and chooses East. Set `Order.AccountId` to Cedar Vale, `Order.SalesStoreId` to the demo store, and copy Shared Billing’s address into the `Order.Billing*` fields. `Order.BillToContactId` and `Order.ShipToContactId` are available to carry Priya and Nora where the order-capture path supports populating them. Link `OrderDeliveryGroup.OrderId` to the order and store East’s name and address in `DeliverToCompanyName` and the `DeliverTo*` address fields. Persist the selected sales area and the actual East, Shared Billing, and Harbor Treasury account identities in a proposed order partner record or in the integrated SAP order, with a reference back to Salesforce. The Commerce order model uses contact and address fields for these values. Those fields alone do not preserve the three partner account identities. [Salesforce Order](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/object-reference/references/standard-objects/object-order.html), [OrderDeliveryGroup fields](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/field-reference/references/field-reference/salesforce-field-reference-orderdeliverygroup.html), [SAP sales-order partners](https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_CLOUD/03c04db2a7434731b7fe21dca77440da/89d75897601d428b8c4bf89b3fd49610.html)

## Acceptance checks and open decisions

- **Defaults and order history.** First confirm that checkout opens with the expected saved address when `BuyerAccount.SendToId` is blank. Then start with fresh carts. Direct should offer North and East and default to North. Contract should offer and default to East. A Direct order with East selected should carry East’s customer ID and delivery address, Shared Billing’s customer ID and billing address, and Harbor Treasury’s customer ID as payer. Confirm the master still defaults Direct to North and that a later master change leaves the submitted order intact. Use fresh carts because Salesforce can retain a previously copied address on `CartDeliveryGroup`. [Checkout address behavior](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=002479955&language=en_US&type=1)
- **Cross-account access.** As Maya, verify that selecting East as a delivery destination leaves Cedar Vale as the effective buyer and gives Maya no access to East’s carts or orders. If multi-account contacts are in scope, first verify the setting and `AccountContactRelation` availability, then check the indirect relationship and repeat the access test. If East also purchases independently, use a separate Buyer Manager with Customer Community Plus or higher. Link both buying accounts to buyer groups assigned to the same store and verify a `DelegatedAccount` with `ManagedById` set to the manager, `TargetId` set to East’s account ID, and `AccessBuyFor=true`, plus explicit read sharing. Verify that East appears and an ungranted account stays inaccessible. Runtime tests remain pending. [Grant buyers access to external accounts](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=commerce.comm_buy_on_behalf.htm&language=en_US&type=5), [DelegatedAccount fields](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/field-reference/references/field-reference/salesforce-field-reference-delegatedaccount.html)
- **Decisions still open.** Agree who owns customer, address, contact, and partner data and in which direction each syncs. Decide where partner determination runs, which defaults buyers may override, how SAP confirms or rejects a selection, and which clinics need independent buyer access. The two sales areas, account hierarchy, and default rules above are example choices for review.
